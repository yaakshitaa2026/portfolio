01 / LIVE DASHBOARD
AI · CYBERSECURITY · IOTPROJECT 01

SafeLab Sentinel

SafeLab Sentinel is an AI-powered laboratory monitoring and cybersecurity system designed to improve lab safety through real-time sensor monitoring, anomaly detection, and device authentication.

Built with Streamlit, FastAPI, and a Random Forest machine-learning model, it analyses temperature, air quality index (AQI), and vibration to identify potential hazards. HMAC-based message integrity checks, risk assessment, and SQLite event logging help flag suspicious device communications and preserve an event history. Simulated normal, hazardous, and cyberattack modes demonstrate how AI, cybersecurity, and industrial safety can work together.

02 / RESEARCH WORKFLOW
MACHINE LEARNING · MATERIALS SCIENCEPROJECT 02

Explainable ML for Carbon-Fibre Composites

This research project applies machine learning to predict and optimise the mechanical properties of carbon-fibre/Polyethersulfone (PSU) composite yarns. Using a dataset of 663 tensile-test specimens, it investigates how fibre concentration, specimen dimensions, mass, die size, processing load, and pre-tension influence tensile strength and related properties.

Multiple models, including Random Forest regression, are being explored to improve prediction accuracy. Explainable AI techniques help identify influential factors, while planned work includes failure-pattern classification and multi-objective optimisation to maximise strength and stiffness while minimising material mass. The project combines materials science, data analytics, and AI to support more informed composite design. I am working under a professor at VIT Vellore, with the aim of contributing to a research paper for publication.
